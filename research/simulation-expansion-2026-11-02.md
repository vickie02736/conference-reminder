# Molecular and atomistic simulation coverage expansion — 2 November 2026

This review adds 33 dated activities and six pending announcements to the existing catalogue. The final catalogue contains 137 dated activities and 12 pending entries. This is a research snapshot, with the UK and Europe prioritised and relevant worldwide meetings included; it is not a claim of exhaustive coverage.

## Scope and inclusion

The review covered classical molecular dynamics, Monte Carlo methods, enhanced/rare-event sampling, free energies and kinetics, molecular and quantum chemistry, atomistic and multiscale modelling, biomolecular simulations, and selected liquid/soft-matter meetings. Schools, practical courses and software-user events are included because the catalogue already supports them. Vendor-led training and broad adjacent meetings are identified in each entry. Closed calls remain visible with their actual status.

Only a published edition is eligible. Dates are not extrapolated from an annual or biennial cycle. Official organisers, societies, universities and software communities take precedence over aggregators. A future deadline does not establish that its submission or registration platform is open. No clock time or time zone is filled from convention alone.

- [CECAM programme](https://www.cecam.org/program), indexed school/conference listings and individual workshop pages; [Psi-k workshops](https://psi-k.net/workshops/); [CECAM-IT-SIMUL](https://cecamsimul.eu/events/events-2026); [ESI](https://www.esi.ac.at/events/).
- [CCP5](https://www.ccp5.ac.uk/), [CCPBioSim upcoming events](https://www.ccpbiosim.ac.uk/events/upcoming-events), BioExcel announcements, Thomas Young Centre, HITS/SIMPLAIX, BMSS and CINECA training. Targeted searches included GROMACS, LAMMPS and PLUMED.
- [Gordon Research Conferences search](https://www.grc.org/find-a-conference/): the public search returned 776 records across 2026–2027, which were screened for relevant future series; individual selected GRC/GRS pages were then read. This does not imply a detailed review of every GRC programme.
- RSC/Faraday Discussions and MGMS announcements; FOMMS, ACTC, WATOC, MM27 and other existing catalogue series were checked for duplication rather than added again.
- EMBO/EMBL, the Biophysical Society calendar and its thematic meetings, IOP Physics of Life, Indian Biophysical Society, the Virtual Winter School, CCG events, ISTCP, Liquid Matter Conference, and the Frontiers in Water Biophysics organiser.
- Broad web searches for the above topics and 2027–2028, followed by primary-source checks. The research did not turn generic aggregator listings into verified meetings.

## Verification limits and decisions

- CECAM's current programme returned an empty event list and its members API returned HTTP 403. Some indexed official announcements contain dates that the current live detail page omits. MolSim 2027, the FLEUR tutorial, the amorphous-fracture workshop and the CECAM–ESI school therefore stay pending, with their indexed dates explained in notes and reminders paused. Existing unrelated CECAM records were not rewritten by this expansion.
- Computational Chemistry Day explicitly announces spring 2027 without dates or venue. It stays pending and has no assigned geographical region (stored under Other).
- TYC references a Sampling with Agents hackathon on 8 December 2026, but no dedicated current organiser/participation page was located. It is a pending lead, separate from the verified 9 December TYC session.
- BMSS's overview gives 12 January 2027 for registration; its training booking page gives 15 January with an inconsistent weekday. The combined training/symposium dates are verified, but its registration milestone is a conflict and cannot be selected for reminders.
- Frontiers in Water Biophysics explicitly gives **6–11 May 2027** on the organiser's current site; external university calendars saying July were not used to override it.
- The RSC molecular-catalysis page was readable through the web reader, but direct HTTP returned 403. Its supported facts are recorded while automatic source checking remains labelled unverifiable.
- ICQC 2026 and past CCP5/other editions were not added as future events. No unannounced 2027 LAMMPS/PLUMED/CCP5/BioExcel dates were invented. Ordinary semester modules and routine short seminars were excluded. Generic conference-alert listings without a separately corroborated specialist programme/academic organiser were not promoted.
- GRC/GRS are separate meetings with separate applications. CCG's four-day user meetings already include their workshop days; BMSS's two days and CCG Cambridge's two training days are grouped to avoid counting the same programme twice.

## Monitoring

The new official URLs are registered in `data/source-checks.json` using the existing fingerprint implementation and the same wider readable-body scope for people sources as the daily updater. Manual source reviews record their actual evidence and timestamp; fetching a page alone is not treated as semantic verification. This was a partial review, so the global last-checked timestamp was not advanced to imply a fresh check of the whole catalogue.

The discovery configuration gains focused queries for MD/atomistic modelling, enhanced sampling/free energies/coarse graining, biomolecular simulation/software, and specialist community calendars. Discovery still produces leads for review, not automatic catalogue additions.
